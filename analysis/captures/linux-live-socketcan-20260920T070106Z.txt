Conclusion: a channel opened for 29-bit identifiers receives none of this bus's 11-bit
traffic, while an 11-bit channel receives it at 2457 frames/s. Receive is filtered by the
channel's identifier width; transmit is not (a transmit carries its own width). So one
channel does NOT deliver both widths, and a 29-bit car needs --29bit.
This car carries no 29-bit traffic at all (10 minutes, 69 identifiers, zero extended),
so what a 29-bit channel does with real 29-bit frames is still unobserved.
