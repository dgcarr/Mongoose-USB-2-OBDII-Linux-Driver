# H12: more than one periodic message on the wire at once. VALIDATION.md listed this as not exercised
# on the car: step 11 only ever ran one. Two read-only OBD-II requests to 0x7DF at different periods
# (mode 01 PID 00 at 1000 ms, mode 01 PID 0C at 500 ms), pass filters for the two ECUs that answer.
# Expect both cadences visible at once, and stopping one to leave the other running.
open dev
connect dev CAN NONE 500000 chan
filter chan PASS CAN NONE 000007ff 000007e8 pass8
filter chan PASS CAN NONE 000007ff 000007e9 pass9
periodic chan CAN NONE 000007df0201005555555555 1000 slow
periodic chan CAN NONE 000007df02010c5555555555 500 fast
readfor chan 16 300 5
mark stopping-the-fast-one
stopperiodic chan fast
readfor chan 16 300 4
mark stopping-the-slow-one
stopperiodic chan slow
readfor chan 16 300 2
stopfilter chan pass9
stopfilter chan pass8
disconnect chan
close dev
